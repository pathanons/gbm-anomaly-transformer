\section{Introduction}

Financial markets generate high-dimensional, noisy, and regime-sensitive time series. Detecting anomalous behavior in such data is important for risk monitoring, event analysis, and market surveillance. However, purely data-driven anomaly detectors may capture temporal patterns without explicitly reflecting the distributional assumptions commonly used in financial modeling.

This paper studies a GBM-aware Transformer for pooled multi-ticker financial anomaly detection. The central idea is to score a window as anomalous when its learned representation, reconstructed OHLCV dynamics, and GBM-implied return distribution become mutually inconsistent.

The intended contributions are:

\begin{itemize}
  \item A Transformer-based framework that combines reconstruction error with GBM-implied return distribution consistency.
  \item A leakage-aware experimental protocol for financial anomaly detection using separate training, validation, and test splits.
  \item An operational score-change threshold analysis that highlights the precision-sensitivity trade-off of conservative alerting.
\end{itemize}

We deliberately avoid claiming baseline superiority until repeated baseline experiments and statistical tests are completed.
