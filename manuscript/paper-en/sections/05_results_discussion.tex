\section{Results and Discussion}

\subsection{Current Evidence}

Current curated evidence supports the dataset construction and motivates the GBM-aware formulation. Score-change thresholding has shown a high-precision, low-sensitivity profile, which may be useful for conservative operational alerts.

\subsection{Limitations}

The current evidence is not sufficient to claim that the proposed model outperforms established baselines. Publication-ready claims require repeated runs, confidence intervals, significance tests, and a complete baseline study. Early-warning claims further require lead-time or detection-delay analysis.
